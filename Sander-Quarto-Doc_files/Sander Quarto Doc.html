Description of data obtention and cleaning

To get the education data from the census bureau: Select all of the counties as variables, pick the variable you want to pick (in this case pick the education attainment) and download the data for all the years you are interested in. When downloading in R, there is one dataset per year.In order to clean the data, get rid of all the variables and specifications only to keep educational attainment for bachelors and more for people aged 25 and over. The specify the nature of the variables, county and state are characters, and the rest are numerical. finally when all datasets per year are cleaned, merge with the column (state, county), this ensures that every state is the correct key, as there are counties that have the same name in separate states. When the sets are merged, separate state and county in separate columns, pivot the data to be in long format (4 observations per county per variable (there are 4 years being analysed)), and rename the variable as needed (in our case Bacmore).

Description of variables

variables of Interest

  • Adult smoking (in percentage of total of population of adults): This variable describes the reported rate of current daily smokers in the population of the county.

  • Excessive drinking: This variable describes the self-reported rate of excessive drinkers of the population in the county.

  • Teen births: This variable describes the amount of teen births per 1000 females aged 15-19.

Main variable

  • Education level (percent of 25 > year olds that have a Bachelors degree minimum): This variable describes the percentage of 25 year olds or older that have a diploma for a Bachelors degree or higher.

Control variables to complete model:

  • Unemployment: This variable describes the unemployment rate in the county. Unemployed people are 16 years old or older and are actively looking for work.

  • Income Inequality: This variable describes the income inequality levels, computed as the ratio of income at the 80th percentile over the income at the 20th percentile.

  • Severe Housing Problems: this variable describes the percentage of households that have at least one of the 4 reported housing problems: high housing costs, overcrowding, lack of kitchen or plumbing facilities.

  • Children in single parent households: This variable describes the percentage of children that grow up in single parent households currently.

  • Mental health providers: This variable describes the rate of mental health providers per 100000 inhabitants.

Research question description (500 words):

The topic we are going to analyse focuses on studying different health habits based on the educational attainment in an observed area, in this case, a county. The variables that will be looked into are adult smoking, excessive drinking, and teen births per 1000 15-19 year old women. We have chosen these variables because these are in someway based on choices that individuals make, rather than an unlucky occurrence in someones life. This means that, for example, getting sick can be the result of bad choices, however someone can not decide to become sick from one day to the other. On the contrary, choosing to smoke, choosing to excessively drink or delivering a baby during teen years are more closely related to choices and thus the environment. This is why we believe it is important to look into the determinants of these habits. One of the possible determinants is educational attainment because it not only influences individuals in their own lives, it also influences individuals in a community. Educational Attainment is computed as the proportion of people in a county that have obtained a Bachelors degree at least. The goal will be to analyse the aggregate decisions of individuals on health habits based on the aggregated educational attainment in a county and compare between more and less educated counties to see whether there are disparities based on educational attainment. Maralani V. (2014) advances that education on its own does not lead to better health, rather, the bundle of educational inequality and environment plays a much larger role in predicting whether someone will smoke. This research will thus try and adapt these findings further on smoking and adapting it to other health habits. The estimation will be completed with different control variables, which we believe can isolate the impact of educational attainment. these are: - Children in single parent households - mental health providers - Severe Housing Problems - Income inequality - Unemployment The vast amount of counties in this dataset, spread through the 51 states of the USA, allow for a large sample size, and thus the development of different conclusions, whether some choices are regionally based, due to a certain education level, or completely random.
